NOTE 001 · A NEW BEGINNING

A more personal space

This website used to introduce my work. I’m giving it a different purpose: a place for the person behind it.

The old version had my education, technical skills, and a professional introduction. It covered what I’d studied and worked on, but it left little space for anything else.

When I started thinking about updating it, I wanted to change more than the layout. I wanted a personal website that could hold writing and bits of life, without every section needing to explain my career.

Starting small

So this version begins with a hello, a little about me, and this first note. I’m keeping the writing in English and leaving room for the site to take shape over time.

I don’t have a collection of essays ready to fill it. This is the beginning of that space, with one short entry about why it exists.

What comes next

For now, I’m working on the basics: a photo that feels more personal, words that sound like me, and a comfortable place to read them.

The rest can arrive one note at a time.

Thanks for being here.
Leon
